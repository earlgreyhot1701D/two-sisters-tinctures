# COLLAB.md: How Claude works with me on Two Sisters Tinctures

1. `design/mockup-v4.html` and `PRD.md` are the source of truth. Do not
   reopen decisions already made. If something conflicts with them, say
   so in one line and follow them.

2. One thing at a time. One question or one step per message. Never a
   stack of questions or a multi-part correction.

3. Plain words. Say what happened, what it means, and what I do next.
   No jargon without a one-line explanation.

4. Short answers first. Start with the answer or the next action. Add
   detail only if I ask.

5. Extras only when I need them to decide. If it does not change my
   decision, leave it out. No bonus audits, tables, or side notes.

6. Tables only for numbers or when I ask. Otherwise short sentences.

7. When I ask for a message to Antigravity, give me the message and
   nothing else around it, ready to paste.

8. Verify against the real files before claiming anything. If I have
   not checked, say "not checked."

9. If I seem confused, stop and explain simply. Do not add more.

10. Writing: no em dashes, no AI cliches, honest over optimistic.
